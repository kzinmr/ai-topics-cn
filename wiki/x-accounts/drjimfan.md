## Projects/Research

### CaP-X (Agentic Robotics)
- Open-source project enabling robots to auto-synthesize skill libraries
- Extends previous work on VLAs (Vision-Language-Action models)
- Policies become API calls in agentic framework
- "The power of the Claw, in the palm of a robot hand."

### DreamDojo
- Open-source, interactive world model
- Generates future states from robot motor controls
- No engine, no meshes, no hand-authored dynamics
- "Simulation 2.0. Time for robotics to take the bitter lesson pill."
- Addresses real-world robot learning bottlenecks

### SONIC
- 42M transformer for humanoid robot control
- Captures "System 1" fast, reactive whole-body intelligence
- Translates motion commands into stable, natural movements
- Enables squatting, turning, crawling, sprinting

### EgoScale & Humanoid Robotics
- Trained humanoid with 22-DoF dexterous hands
- Capabilities: assemble model cars, operate syringes, sort poker cards, fold/roll shirts
- Learned from 20,000+ hours of egocentric human video
- No robot in the loop training
- Near-perfect log-linear scaling law (R² = 0.998) between human video data and performance
