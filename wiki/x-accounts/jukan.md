## 主なトピック・主張

### HBM供給交渉（SK hynix 3Q25決算説明会）

> "This year, numerous external variables made it difficult to finalize not only supply volumes but also product mix. As customer performance requirements evolved, it took longer than initially expected. However, discussions with customers have now been concluded, and supply contracts for next year's HBM have been finalized."

> "Since 2023, demand for AI infrastructure and the company's strong product competitiveness have kept HBM fully sold out. Prices are currently set at a level that allows profitability to be maintained."

> "Given the rapid expansion of HBM demand driven by the AI market, it will be difficult for supply to catch up with demand in the near term. Growth is expected to outpace general DRAM. Even in 2027, supply is projected to remain tight relative to demand."

HBMは2023年から完売状態が続き、2027年も供給逼迫が続くと予測。

### HBM4技術

> "As the AI inference market expands, memory bandwidth has become increasingly important. Since the number of I/O channels in HBM4 has been set to double that of the previous generation, customer requirements have shifted toward higher-speed performance."

HBM4はI/Oチャネル数が倍増し、AI推論市場の拡大に伴いメモリバンド幅が重要になっていると分析。

### メモリスーパーサイクル論

> "Contrary to earlier expectations, memory demand has surged this year, pushing the market into a super-boom. We believe the current cycle differs from the 2017/2018 cycle. The key difference is that today's demand is tied to a paradigm shift toward AI."

現在のメモリサイクルは2017/2018年のサイクルとは異なり、AIへのパラダイムシフトに紐づいた構造的な需要拡大だと主張。

### eSSD需要の構造的拡大

> "As AI-generated data such as images and videos surges, storage needs are climbing, creating HDD supply shortages. Hyperscalers are increasingly shifting to high-capacity QLC eSSDs."

> "To overcome the limits of the traditional LLM approach, the importance of RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is growing. Implementing RAG requires building external database vector stores, and eSSDs are essential for fast retrieval."

RAGの普及により外部データベース用ベクトルストアの需要が増加し、eSSD需要が構造的に拡大すると分析。

### KVキャッシュのSSDオフロード

> "By offloading the GPU's KV cache down to SSDs, it's possible to improve throughput per watt and reduce user response latency."

GPUのKVキャッシュをSSDにオフロードすることで、ワットあたりのスループット向上と応答遅延の削減が可能と指摘。

### 半導体サイクル（Citi分析の紹介）

> "Citi analyst Christopher Danely expects global semiconductor sales to rise 16% in 2025 to reach an all-time high of $731 billion. However, he emphasized that this revenue growth is entirely driven by pricing, while shipment volumes remain well below previous peaks."

Citiアナリストの分析を紹介し、半導体売上高の成長が価格主導であり、出荷量は過去のピークを大きく下回っていると指摘。在庫水準が低く、業界にはさらなる成長余地があると論じた。

### Apple DRAM LTA問題

iPhone Wiredの報道によると、AppleがサムスンおよびSKハイニクスと締結しているDRAM長期供給契約（LTA）の期限切れに直面しており、時価総額4兆ドルのAppleですら現在の半導体供給逼迫から逃れられない状況だと報じた。
