### AIサーバー用PMIC分析

> "PMIC is a particularly notable case. Because AI servers require far higher power density than general-purpose servers, ASPs are higher, incentivizing suppliers to prioritize AI PMIC production. Consequently, 8-inch wafer BCD (Bipolar-CMOS-DMOS) process capacity is increasingly being allocated to AI PMICs."

AIサーバーの電力密度要件の高まりが、PMIC（電源管理IC）供給に与える構造的な影響を分析。8インチBCDプロセスキャパシティのAI PMICへの振り分けが進行していると指摘。
