The interesting part isn’t the speed itself.
This robot doesn’t even need a head module—it runs just fine without it. Motion control and vision are fully decoupled: IMU sits in the torso, force sensors are in the legs. Losing the head is just “losing a screen.”
This fundamentally differs from humans and shows how
redundancy in robotics is redefining what counts as a “critical component.”
