Even traditional electroplating processes are being disrupted by startups—and one of them comes from Australia. Syenta has developed a new electrochemical process capable of producing narrower redistribution layer (RDL) line widths than existing technologies, improving the performance of advanced packaging.
Of all the companies I encountered, this was the one that impressed me most. It demonstrates just how deeply the sweeping momentum to reinvent every aspect of AI hardware has penetrated the industry.
