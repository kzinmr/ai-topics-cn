# Anthropic顶级模型Mythos遭非法访问

**Source:** 36kr (财联社AI daily)
**Date:** 2026-04-23
**URL:** https://36kr.com/p/3778777712235782
**Categories:** [anthropic, mythos, security, unauthorized access]

## Summary
Unauthorized users accessed Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview model — the strongest model, not yet ready for public release. The model can identify and exploit vulnerabilities across major operating systems and browsers. Access was obtained through a private Discord group using third-party contractor permissions and data from the Mercor data breach.

## Key Points
- Mythos Preview was limited to select tech/finance partners due to capability concerns
- Unauthorized access via: third-party contractor permissions + Mercor data leak + network scanning
- Users are in a private Discord group doing simple web development (not malicious)
- Anthropic response: investigating, no evidence of access beyond third-party environment
- Japanese Finance Minister discussing Mythos with major banks (MUFG, SMBC, Mizuho)
- Australian Reserve Bank monitoring the situation

## Analysis
This is the first known case of unauthorized access to a pre-release frontier AI model with cybersecurity capabilities. The incident highlights the risk of "model preview" access even in controlled environments.
