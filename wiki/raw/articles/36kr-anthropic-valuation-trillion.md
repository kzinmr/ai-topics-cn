# Anthropic估值暴涨破万亿美元，首次超越OpenAI

**Source:** 36kr (新智元)
**Date:** 2026-04-23
**URL:** https://36kr.com/p/3778903190639617
**Categories:** [anthropic, openai, valuation, Claude Code, secondary market]

## Summary
Anthropic's private secondary market valuation approaches $1 trillion, surpassing OpenAI (~$88B) on Forge Global and similar platforms. Key drivers: scarce share supply, revenue growth, and Claude Code's product momentum. Capital is re-evaluating AI companies based on "entry point control and commercial realization" rather than model rankings.

## Key Points
- Anthropic: ~$1T on secondary markets (sellers asking $1.05T-$1.15T)
- OpenAI: ~$88B on same platforms (down from $852B in March financing)
- Anthropic's last round was ~$380B three months ago — 2-3x increase
- Claude Code positions Anthropic as infrastructure company, not just model company
- AI valuation logic shifting from "who has stronger models" to "who controls developer/enterprise entry points"
- Forge Global amplifies price momentum by aggregating scattered buyers/sellers

## Analysis
This represents a fundamental shift in AI industry valuation framework. The market now values platform position and revenue predictability over raw model capability. Claude Code's integration into developer workflows gives Anthropic a defensible moat that investors are pricing at a premium.
