Hello everyone,
I have always thought that AblePrint Technology（印能） is a rather unusual company.
Industry insiders used to lament the lack of new blood in Taiwan’s technology sector. Among the major semiconductor companies, Phison（群聯） was the only one founded in or after 2000 (in that very year, in fact).
The situation was even more pronounced among equipment makers. Then came AblePrint, founded in 2007, which broke with convention by taking “just” 17 years to go public on Taiwan’s Emerging Stock Market, where it became the highest-priced stock.
Founder Chih-Hung Hung（洪誌宏） made his first public appearance at the company’s pre-listing investor conference in 2024. With his gray-and-white hair, he unexpectedly showed up wearing a black leather jacket reminiscent of the godfather of AI, prompting the media to exclaim: “A Jensen Huang lookalike!”
Since then, I have occasionally heard stories about “Chairman Hung” solving long-standing packaging process problems for certain AI chips, enabling their manufacturers to finally achieve mass production.
An executive at one of Taiwan’s 10 largest technology companies by market capitalization told me that while Taiwanese semiconductor equipment makers have generally ridden the CoWoS supply chain boom to prosperity, “Chih-Hung Hung is the only one who really knows his stuff.”
That made me curious about him.
Some time ago, a chance opportunity finally allowed me to have an in-depth conversation with this rising star of the semiconductor equipment industry.
