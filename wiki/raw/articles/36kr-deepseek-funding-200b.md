# 曝两家科技大厂争投DeepSeek — 估值飙至200亿美元

**Source:** 36kr (智东西)
**Date:** 2026-04-22
**URL:** https://36kr.com/p/3778031529726983
**Categories:** [deepseek, funding, valuation, tencent, alibaba]

## Summary
DeepSeek seeking $200B+ valuation for external funding round. Tencent and Alibaba in negotiations. Initial target was $100B+ (April 17), now doubling to $200B+. DeepSeek has never generated significant revenue — operates as an open-source AI lab, free chatbot. Previously refused external funding; founder Liang Wenfeng insisted on independent operation.

## Key Points
- Tencent and Alibaba negotiating to invest in DeepSeek
- Valuation: $200B+ (~¥1.36 trillion), up from $100B target on April 17
- Moon of Darkness (月之暗面/Kimi): ~$180B next round
- Competitors MiniMax and Zhipu: ~$100B at Hong Kong IPO (Jan)
- Zhipu market cap: ~$438.6B HKD; MiniMax: ~$291.1B HKD
- DeepSeek's operational model as open-source lab makes valuation controversial
- Key researchers have left for Chinese tech giants
- New capital needed for compute resources and talent retention

## Analysis
DeepSeek's valuation represents a fundamental disagreement between its open-source ethos and traditional VC valuation metrics. The $200B figure is speculative and likely driven by FOMO rather than fundamentals.
