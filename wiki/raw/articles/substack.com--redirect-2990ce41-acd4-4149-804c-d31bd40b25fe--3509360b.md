# Beyond Wukong: China’s First Fully AI-Generated Series Hits Prime Time

Source: https://substack.com/redirect/2990ce41-acd4-4149-804c-d31bd40b25fe?j=eyJ1IjoiMmZseDYifQ.6O_iC1_GWCzChnbrHzSrs1hMpJpoODTUJm_FAk3tf6E

If you thought the AI wave was just a passing trend, think again. Remember when
we originally reported
on the much-talked-about full AI-generated series based on
Journey to the West
? Well, it’s officially here, and it’s making history.
Titled
The Later Journey to the West
(or
Beyond Wukong
), this visually polished reimagining of the Ming Dynasty classic premiered in prime time on Hunan Satellite Television and
Mango TV
this September. Here at RADII, we’re no strangers to the intersection of tech and culture. We’ve been tracking AI-generated content for a minute (funnily enough, especially
Monkey King-related projects
). But
Beyond Wukong
serves as a massive move for China. It’s the first-ever fully AI-generated entertainment to hit mainstream provincial satellite prime time.
Image via MangoTV.
Set a millennium after Sun
Wukong
’s legendary triumph, the synopsis follows a young stone monkey named Sun Xiaosheng and his crew on a fresh pilgrimage to redeem the cosmic order. The show breaks conventional remake formulas by offering striking, traditional Chinese painting-style visuals that blend technological innovation with traditional oriental aesthetics.
But what about the human touch? Directed by Li Dongshen, the production team behind the series insists on a human-centered creative philosophy. While AI fundamentally altered the workflow—handling visual creation and making plot iterations incredibly fluid—all core storylines, character growth, and emotional beats were exclusively polished by human screenwriters. To avoid copyright risks and preserve authenticity, the crew also adopted human dubbing over AI voice synthesis.
Image via Facebook/Bridging News.
The premiere scored strong debut viewership but also sparked heated debate on Chinese social media. Fans are praising the digital rejuvenation of a classic IP, while critics question whether
AI
can capture the profound cultural connotations of classic literature. Whether you’re here for the tech or the tradition,
Beyond Wukong
has officially ushered AIGC into the mainstream.
