# X 的 Premium 订阅不给退款

Premium 申请退款遭到拒绝：

Hello,

Thank you for being an X customer. After further review, this scenario is ineligible for a refund according to our Purchaser Terms of Service.

Your support case is now closed, and this email is not monitored for replies. 

For any further assistance, check out Premium FAQs or feel free to contact @Premium for support.

Thank you,

X Support
